The idea

Good visualization doesn't need to be complicated.

A useful Digital Twin should make a complicated place easier to understand, not add another layer of complexity.

I focus on the visual core: the structures, spaces, and relationships people actually need to see — then build them efficiently enough to work in a lightweight online environment.

A little about me

Maps, models
& real places.

I'm a 3D modeler and GIS analyst with 20+ years of experience in the AEC and geospatial world. I like taking complicated physical environments, figuring out what matters, and making them understandable.

My work sits somewhere between GIS, 3D modeling, visualization, and problem-solving. I work quickly, pay attention to details, and prefer solving the right problem over building the most complicated solution.
