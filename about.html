About / Pavel Belsky

Spatial Data & 3D Environments

I build digital twins, smart space management systems, and historical 3D reconstructions by bridging geographic data with architectural visualization.

Working across Esri ArcGIS, QGIS, Blender, Revit, and custom WebGIS solutions, my focus centers on making complex spatial information intuitive, interactive, and actionable for infrastructure, municipal, and heritage applications.

"Translating raw spatial data into living digital environments."

Core Capabilities

Digital Twin Development

City-wide context massing, port facility modeling, multi-LOD asset pipelines, and live operational data integration.

Smart Space & Facilities

Indoor spatial navigation, room-level inventory tracking, BIM-to-GIS integration, and facility management dashboards.

Historical Preservation

Archival perspective rectification, chronological site evolution renders, and virtual reconstructions of lost landmarks.

Spatial Data Automation

Python script workflows for OpenStreetMap data extraction, custom SketchUp Ruby scripts, and procedural material creation.
